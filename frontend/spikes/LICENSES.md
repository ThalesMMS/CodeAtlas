# Dependency licenses

Pinned dependency licenses are captured by `benchmarks/run-benchmarks.ts` in the
versioned result JSON. As of this spike, the direct editor/test/build
dependencies use these licenses:

- Monaco Editor: MIT
- CodeMirror 6 packages: MIT
- Vite: MIT
- Playwright: Apache-2.0
- axe-core: MPL-2.0
- tsx: MIT
- TypeScript: Apache-2.0
